BLUESHORES was presented by Laura Airoldi at the kick-off meeting of the projects co-funded under the 2024 Joint Transnational Call of the Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership (SBEP), on 12 February 2026.

The presentation introduced the project's vision for a new generation of hybrid blue-green infrastructure, integrating soft and hard eco-engineering elements to deliver multifunctional, cost-effective foreshore protection. Inspired by local heritage, biomimicry and circular economy principles, the proposed solutions aim to enhance coastal resilience while generating environmental, societal and blue economy benefits.

The presentation highlighted:

- The challenges associated with traditional coastal protection infrastructure and the opportunities offered by hybrid nature-based approaches.
- The three demonstration sites: Venice Lagoon, the Eastern Scheldt Estuary and Cork Harbour.
- The development of quality standards, validated designs, numerical modelling tools and guidelines for scaling up.
- Progress in stakeholder engagement, site selection, design adaptation and baseline environmental monitoring.
- The planned outreach and dissemination activities, including an itinerant, interactive BLUESHORES exhibition.

Being part of the SBEP community offers valuable opportunities for collaboration with related European projects, shared learning, methodological alignment and a stronger connection between science, society and policy.

The full presentation is now available for download, providing an overview of the project's objectives, methodology, consortium, ongoing activities and expected impacts.
