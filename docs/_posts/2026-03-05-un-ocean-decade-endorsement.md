We are delighted to announce that BLUESHORES has been officially endorsed by the Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission of UNESCO (IOC-UNESCO) as a Decade Action contributing to the United Nations Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development (2021–2030).

This international recognition marks an important milestone for BLUESHORES and acknowledges the project's contribution to developing science-based solutions for sustainable and resilient coastal environments.

BLUESHORES develops and tests hybrid blue-green foreshore infrastructure designed to protect vulnerable shorelines while restoring habitats, supporting biodiversity, reducing reliance on carbon-intensive materials, and generating sustainable blue economy opportunities.

Field experiments, stakeholder engagement, environmental monitoring, numerical modelling and cost-benefit assessment, are central activities in the project to generate evidence and practical guidance for scaling up innovative coastal protection solutions across Europe and beyond.

As an endorsed Decade Action, BLUESHORES is committed to advancing the Ocean Decade principles of co-design, stakeholder participation, open and accessible data, knowledge sharing and collaboration with the international ocean science community.

The endorsement provides new opportunities to establish synergies with other Ocean Decade initiatives, and together, we contribute to the Ocean Decade vision of **"the science we need for the ocean we want."**
