As part of Science for All, the University of Padua’s public science outreach event, the BLUESHORES immersive exhibition was inaugurated at Palazzo Grassi, home of the Giuseppe Olivi Museum in Chioggia.

The exhibition invites visitors to experience the lagoon through photographs, artistic installations, natural materials and audio elements. It introduces the challenges facing coastal ecosystems and the project’s exploration of hybrid approaches to coastal protection and habitat restoration.

Developed in collaboration with researchers from the Department of General Psychology at the University of Padua, the exhibition also explores how visitors perceive and respond emotionally to natural environments, including their potential restorative qualities.

The exhibition contributes to BLUESHORES' objective to make coastal research accessible to a wider public and encourage reflection on the relationship between people and the lagoon.

The exhibition initiative runs from September 2026 to January 2027.
