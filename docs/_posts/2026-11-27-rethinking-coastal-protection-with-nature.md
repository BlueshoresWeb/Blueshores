Coastal communities across Europe are facing increasing pressure from erosion, flooding, biodiversity loss, and climate change. Traditional grey infrastructure has an important role to play, but it can be costly, carbon-intensive, and limited in the ecological and social benefits it provides.

BLUESHORES is developing and testing a new generation of hybrid blue-green infrastructure: flexible coastal protection solutions that combine engineered elements with nature-based restoration.

The project will test innovative combinations of:

- biodegradable elements to support saltmarsh recovery
- oyster-based reefs to help restore habitat and attenuate waves
- wood fascine breakwalls inspired by local coastal practices

These solutions will be tested in [three European coastal systems]({{ "/case-studies/" | relative_url }}): Venice Lagoon, Eastern Scheldt Estuary, and Cork Harbour.

Our objective is to demonstrate how coastal protection can also support biodiversity, reduce environmental impact, improve landscape value, and provide practical tools for planners, authorities, infrastructure operators, and coastal communities.
