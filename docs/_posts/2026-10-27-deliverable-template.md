We are pleased to launch the new Blueshores website. It brings together what the project aims to achieve, how we are testing hybrid multifunctional foreshore infrastructure in Venice Lagoon, the Eastern Scheldt and Cork Harbour, and the latest news from the team.

**What you will find here**

- **Objectives**: the goals and approach of the project.
- **Case Studies**: an interactive overview of our three sites, with their constraints, interventions and monitoring.
- **News**: project updates, added here as the work progresses.

More material, including figures of the installations at each site, will be added over time.
