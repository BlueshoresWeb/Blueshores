On 30 June 2026, BLUESHORES held a full-day stakeholder engagement event in Cork, Ireland, bringing together local communities and key stakeholders to exchange knowledge and explore the future of coastal and wetland ecosystems.

The workshop introduced the project’s approach to hybrid blue-green infrastructure and created space for participants to share their experiences, perspectives and expertise. Discussions focused on local challenges and priorities for coastal protection, wetland restoration and sustainable management.

Interactive group activities and knowledge-sharing sessions, have animated the day with participants exploring potential approaches to restoration in the local context. The workshop materials helped structure conversations around practical challenges and implementation considerations.

Linking local experience with scientific knowledge is essential to ensure that coastal protection solutions respond not only to environmental conditions but also to the needs of the people who live and work around these ecosystems. The event forms part of BLUESHORES wider commitment to stakeholder engagement and co-design.
